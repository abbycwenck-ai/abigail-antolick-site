About Me

I'm the marketer who got tired of waiting on developers.

After 8+ years running marketing operations for brands generating $10M+ annually — managing teams, building 50+ sales funnels, and launching 46 campaigns a quarter — I kept hitting the same wall: proving ROI meant waiting weeks for a dev to build a report.

So I learned to build dashboards myself. I've migrated CRM infrastructure, built attribution models, and created real-time reporting systems — all without a dev team. Now my clients get strategy AND the tools to see it working.

I work exclusively with startups doing $10M and under because that's where marketing is the most fun — scrappy, fast, and high-impact.
